# Preserved artwork excluded from release

`wine_01.sticker/` is the original, unmodified wine-glass sticker recovered from the 2018 repository. On October 26, 2026, the owner chose to omit it from the shipped pack to remove its alcohol reference and aim for an age rating of 4+.

It is preserved here, outside the native asset catalog, and is not included in the app or Studio gallery. The other ten recovered classics and all 36 new designs remain in the release: **46 stickers total**. Do not re-add the archived wine sticker to the catalog or change the rating answers without an intentional product decision.
