Your privacy

Privacy policy.

Effective date: October 26, 2026

8 bit art is a Messages app provided by mim Armand. It does not require an app-specific account. This policy covers the current sticker pack and the native Studio being prepared for version 1.4.

Information collected by the app

The app does not collect personal information on a developer-operated server. It contains no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. There is no developer account system or cloud artwork service.

The developer does not receive your messages, recipients, contacts, photos, sticker conversations, drawings, favorites, or usage history through the app.

Drawings and favorites on your device

In the forthcoming Studio, drawings, drawing names, and favorites are saved in the app’s storage on your device. They are not uploaded to a developer-operated server or automatically synchronized by Studio between devices.

You control PNG export and insertion into Messages. When you share a drawing, the services and recipients you choose handle that shared copy. Deleting the app can remove its local drawings and favorites, so export work you want to keep. Restoring a purchase does not restore deleted artwork.

Studio Unlock and purchase information

The optional Studio Unlock is a permanent one-time In-App Purchase. Apple handles checkout, payment, purchase history, and restoration. The app uses StoreKit to read product information and verify purchase access on the device. It does not send transaction records to a developer-operated server.

The developer does not receive your payment card details through Studio. Sandbox and TestFlight purchases are tests and do not grant ownership of the public App Store purchase.

Apple services

Downloading the app, buying or restoring Studio Unlock, and using Messages involve Apple services governed by Apple’s own terms and privacy policies. Apple may process account, transaction, and service information. The app does not send private conversations or artwork to the developer.

You can read more in Apple’s privacy information.

If you contact support

If you choose to email support, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use that correspondence to respond to your request and keep it only as needed to handle support and applicable obligations.

Please avoid including sensitive information or private message content. You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any retention required by law.

This support website

This website contains no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies, account system, or upload forms. Following a link to Apple or sending an email involves the services you choose to use.

Our hosting provider may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses, for delivery, security, and reliability. If this site is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details. This website activity is separate from using the Messages app and its device-local drafts.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from users, including children, on a developer-operated server. If you believe personal information has been sent to us inadvertently, please contact support.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if the app’s data practices change. The effective date above identifies this version.

For privacy questions or support requests, contact seltzer.wisdom7h@icloud.com.
