Your privacy

Privacy policy.

Effective date: October 26, 2026

8 bit art is an offline sticker pack for Apple’s Messages app, provided by mim Armand. It does not require an account.

Information collected by the app

The sticker pack does not collect, transmit, or store personal information on a developer-operated server. It contains no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

The developer does not receive your messages, recipients, contacts, photos, sticker conversations, or usage history through the sticker pack.

Apple services

Downloading the app and using Messages involve Apple services governed by Apple’s own terms and privacy policies. Information processed by those services is not sent to the developer by the sticker pack.

You can read more in Apple’s privacy information.

If you contact support

If you choose to email support, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use that correspondence to respond to your request and keep it only as needed to handle support and applicable obligations.

Please avoid including sensitive information or private message content. You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any retention required by law.

This support website

This website contains no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies, account system, or upload forms. Following a link to Apple or sending an email involves the services you choose to use.

Our hosting provider may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses, for delivery, security, and reliability. If this site is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details. This website activity is separate from the offline sticker pack.

Children

The sticker pack does not collect personal information from users, including children. If you believe personal information has been sent to us inadvertently, please contact support.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if the app’s data practices change. The effective date above identifies this version.

For privacy questions or support requests, contact seltzer.wisdom7h@icloud.com.
